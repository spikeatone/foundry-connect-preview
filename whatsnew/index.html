Foundry Connect

Foundry now speaks Spanish.

A large part of the young people Foundry is built for think, talk, and dream in Spanish. So this release brings the whole thing — the app, this site, and the mentor's own voice — into Latin American Spanish. A student who sets their phone to Spanish is met in Spanish, all the way down, and the trusted adult in their corner can read Connect in either language.

August 26, 2026 · Connect and app build 14 · foundryconnect.me
In the Foundry app · build 14

The whole app, in Spanish New

Every screen — the welcome, the interview, your portrait, your paths, the Foundry Index, sharing — now reads in Latin American Spanish when the phone's language is set to Spanish. Nothing is half-translated: a Spanish student sees a Spanish app, start to finish.

It follows the phone: Foundry uses your device language, so there's no setting to hunt for. English stays exactly as it was for everyone else.

The mentor talks with you in Spanish

The mentor isn't translated word-for-word — it genuinely speaks Spanish, the way a good counselor would. It uses tú, keeps the same honesty about real careers and real ranges, and names the actual trades (electricista, técnico, soldador) instead of talking down to anyone. Same warmth, same straight talk, in the language you think in.

Honest in any language: the mentor still refuses to make false promises or fake a number, in Spanish exactly as in English.

Connect speaks Spanish too

This site now asks, the very first time you open it, whether you'd like it in English or Spanish — and there's a toggle in the menu to switch anytime. A parent, counselor, or coach can read a student's shared journey in whichever language is theirs.

Your choice, remembered: Connect keeps your language on this device, so it opens the way you left it.

Testing this build?

Give extra attention to: set your phone to Spanish (Settings → General → Language & Region, or per-app in Foundry's settings) and walk through the welcome, the interview, and your portrait — does it read like natural Spanish, and does the mentor answer you in Spanish? Then open foundryconnect.me, pick Español at the gate, and try the EN/ES toggle in the menu.

Also in this build

  • The Foundry Index, still sorted to you. Everything from the last build stays: in Schools, type your ZIP and sort by Nearest, Lowest cost, or By pay, with an Explore link to each school's own site — now in Spanish too.
  • Honest gaps stay honest. A program that hasn't reported its cost or earnings still sinks to the bottom and says so, in either language, rather than faking a number.
