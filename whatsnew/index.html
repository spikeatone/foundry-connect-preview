The Foundry Index, sorted to the student in front of you.

The Foundry Index is our honest read on technical schools and training programs — ranked on real earnings and completion data, not brochure promises. This release lets a student re-order any list around what matters to them: the closest program to home, the cheapest, or the one that leads to the best pay. And every school now links straight to its own site to go look.

August 26, 2026 · Connect and app build 13 · foundryconnect.me
In the Foundry app · build 13

Sort the Index to what matters to you New

Open any list in Schools and you can re-order it three new ways. Type your ZIP and tap Nearest to bring the closest programs to the top. Tap Lowest cost to lead with the most affordable, or By pay to lead with the work that pays best. The Foundry rank is still there as Best match — these just let a student ask a different question of the same honest data.

Honest when it's blank: a program that hasn't reported its cost or earnings sinks to the bottom and says so, rather than faking a number to look better than it is.

Explore a school in one tap

Every school on the list now carries an Explore link that opens its own website, so a student can go straight from "this looks like a fit" to the school's real programs, dates, and how to apply. No hunting, no dead ends.

Never a dead link: the button only shows where we have a verified address for the school. If it's not there, the space stays honest and empty.

The same Index the counselor sees

This is the shelf that makes Foundry different: the trade schools, apprenticeships, and two-year programs most students never hear about, weighed on what actually happens after — pay, completion, and credential quality — with the reasoning shown. It reads the same in the app and here on Connect, so a student and the adult in their corner are looking at one honest list.

A point of view, not a scoreboard: it's Foundry's read on the public data, never an official government ranking — and a missing stat means the data isn't published, never a zero.

Testing this build?

Give extra attention to: the Schools tab — open a list, type a ZIP and try Nearest, then flip between Lowest cost and By pay (does the order change the way you'd expect?), and tap Explore on a school (does it open the right site?).

Under the hood

  • Cleaner school cards. The stat pills (earnings, completion, exam pass) now wrap neatly instead of cramming, so a long line like "typical pay (BLS)" stays readable.
  • Steadier and quieter. Behind the scenes, the app is more resilient to a hiccup in the data it loads, and reports crashes to us so we can fix them faster — none of which you should ever notice.
