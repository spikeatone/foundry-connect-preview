The window now shows you what moved.

Connect began as a beautiful snapshot: who this young person is, what they chose to share. This release makes it a living window, and gives the app a living memory. Connect tells you what changed and closes the loop when you reach out; the app now remembers, offers the next step, and keeps every chapter of the story.

Coming back is now worth it

Since you last looked New

The recap opens with a quiet line when something changed: "Since you last looked: 1 new experience and 1 new path." Fresh trail entries carry a small NEW marker so your eye lands on what's actually new.

Still theirs: the strip only counts what that student shared with you. An advisor without the trail sees only the path changes.

Notes come full circle

Your sent notes now live under the composer, and when the student opens one in the app, its status turns from Sent to "saw this." A receipt, not a read-timestamp: you know it landed, and that's all.

He just logged "Toured the Cascade Wind training tower". A good week for a short note.

And a well-timed prompt: when something new lands, Connect suggests the moment, so encouragement arrives when it means the most.

A weekly digest, if you want one

Turn on the digest, add your email, pick weekly or monthly. On quiet weeks nothing is sent.

The hard promise: the email only says there is new activity and links you back here. What the student shared never leaves the window.

The Foundry Index, through their lens

Rankings that start from their paths

The Index no longer opens on a national shelf. It leads with "For Jordan's paths": each path they're exploring maps to its published rankings, opened sorted by distance from their area, with a "matches his diesel path" tag so you know why you're looking at it.

Honest gaps stay honest: a path with no published ranking says so plainly, instead of pretending.

Every school row shows its work

Rows now carry the Foundry Score, net price, and median debt beside earnings and completion, plus a plain-language line: "Score parts: earnings 52 · completion 87 · affordability 45 · credential quality 87."

Why it matters: a counselor can defend a suggestion, and a family can see the money picture before falling for a brochure.

Every button goes somewhere real

School cards open their ranking in the Index. Funding cards link to the real doors: apprenticeship.gov, Washington student aid, the FAFSA. And Suggest to Jordan starts a note for you, ready to send.

In the Foundry app · build 9

The mentor remembers you now

Check-ins, nudges, and Portrait conversations draw on your Portrait, your paths, and what you've actually done. Expect replies that sound like someone who was there for the earlier chapters, because now it was.

Paths never run dry

Every reflection can end with one small offered next step, Add it or Not now, always your call. And "Not for me" grew up: say why if you want, set a whole path aside, and it stays part of your story. Crossing something off is progress, not failure.

Time, told honestly

Steps with real deadlines now say so: "Closes Aug 25 · 5 days." Calm, never alarm-red. Home opens with "Since you were last here" when something moved, and unread notes from your people badge the Mentor tab. When you read one, the sender simply sees it landed. That's the other half of the receipt above.

How you've grown

Me now keeps every version of your Portrait, side by side. Who you were at the start does not get overwritten by who you're becoming. Nothing is erased.

Also in this build: a rewritten consent gate on first run. If you've been testing since v1, you'll be asked once more, on purpose.

Testing this build?

Give extra attention to: the reflection to next-step loop (does the offered step feel right?), the "Not for me" flow, and the one-time re-consent prompt after upgrading.
