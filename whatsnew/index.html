Free to start, always New

Nothing worth doing is behind a wall on day one. The interview, your Portrait, your home, and your first path with its full check-in loop are free, for everyone, forever. That's the part that helps a student figure out who they are — and it never costs anything.

No surprise wall: you get all the way to a real read on yourself and a first real step before Foundry ever mentions a subscription.

Foundry+ unlocks the whole journey

When a student's ready for more, Foundry+ opens it up: every path (not just the first), the always-there mentor ("Talk to me"), sharing with the people in your corner through Connect, and the full Foundry Index of ranked schools. It's $49.99 a year with a 1-week free trial — an annual commitment for a mentor that's meant to be there for years, not a monthly churn.

Honest about money too: real price, real trial, cancel anytime in Settings — the same straight-talk the mentor gives you everywhere else.

A code from your school unlocks it all

Schools and districts can hand their students a one-time code that unlocks everything Foundry+ offers — no subscription, no card. On the Me screen, tap Have a code from your school?, enter it, and the whole app opens up. When a school's access ends, the app quietly returns to the free tier — nothing you did is ever deleted.

Built for the whole school: a counselor provisions codes for their students; each one is a private, one-time unlock tied to that student's account.

Then, if you have a school code, try Have a code from your school? on the Me screen.

  • Foundry speaks Spanish, end to end. Set your phone to Spanish and the whole app — the interview, your portrait, the mentor's own voice — meets you in Latin American Spanish. The Foundry Index now shows Spanish school-list titles and details too, and Connect has its own English/Español switch for the family in your corner.
