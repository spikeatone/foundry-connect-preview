Bring the people in your corner along. Sharing is free.

You can now invite a teacher, a counselor or someone in your family by email. They sign in at foundryconnect.me and see what you choose to share, and nothing else. Sharing costs nothing on any surface, and school codes now redeem at app.foundryconnect.me.

September 22, 2026 · iPhone 1.0 (23) · Android build 7 · app.foundryconnect.me
In the Foundry app · iPhone build 23 · Android build 7 · the web

Invite someone by email New

In Who can see me, tap Add someone and give their name, their role and their email. They get an email saying you shared your Foundry journey with them. They sign in at foundryconnect.me with that same address and see only what you've chosen: your Portrait, your paths, your trail, the road ahead.

You stay in charge: nobody sees anything until you add them. When you do, your Portrait and paths are shared to start. Switch any piece off, or remove the person, whenever you want.

New roles: Teacher and Family member New

Alongside Counselor, Parent, Coach and Other, you can now add someone as a Teacher or a Family member, like a grandparent, an aunt or an older cousin. Connect shows each person by the role you picked.

Sharing is free, for everyone New

Bringing the people in your corner along no longer needs Foundry+. Adding someone, choosing what they see and changing your mind are free on iPhone, Android and the web.

Why: the adults helping you figure this out shouldn't be behind a paywall.

School codes now redeem on the web New

If your school gave you a code, go to app.foundryconnect.me and sign in with the same email you use in the app. Open You, type the code under Membership, and tap Redeem. Your account unlocks right away, and the iPhone and Android apps unlock too when you're signed in with that same account. When a school's access ends, Foundry quietly returns to the free tier. Nothing you did is ever deleted.

Heads up: the apps no longer have a code field, so the web is the place to enter it. If you signed in on iPhone with Apple's Hide My Email, the web can't match that account yet. Write to hello@postmarkdigital.com before you redeem, so your code lands on the right account.

Help, right there on a check-in Web

If something you write in a check-in, the interview or a talk with your mentor sounds like you might not be safe, Foundry stops and puts help on the screen: Call or Text 988, the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline, and Call 911, one tap each.

Where: on the web at app.foundryconnect.me today. The iPhone and Android apps get the same card in their next update.

Free to start, always

The interview, your Portrait, your home and your first path with its full check-in loop are free, for everyone. That's the part that helps you figure out who you are, and it never costs anything.

No surprise wall: you get all the way to a real read on yourself and a first real step before Foundry ever mentions a subscription.

Foundry+ unlocks the rest of the journey

When you're ready for more, Foundry+ opens every path (not just the first), the always-there mentor ("Talk to me") and the full Foundry Index of ranked schools. On iPhone and Android it's $49.99 a year, with a 1-week free trial the first time. A school code unlocks the same things at no cost to you.

Honest about money too: the real price up front, and you can cancel anytime in your App Store or Google Play subscriptions.

Testing this build?
