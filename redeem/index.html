Got a Foundry code from your school?

Your school is covering Foundry+ for you. Three steps and you're in. Most of the time goes to the interview, about 10 minutes.

1

Start on the website: do your interview and save it

Open app.foundryconnect.me
  1. Tap Begin and talk with your mentor. It asks about what you like and how you work.
  2. When your Portrait appears, tap Sign in to save your Portrait.
  3. Type your email, then the 6-digit code we email you. We strongly suggest using a personal email address so you can continue to use Foundry after you graduate.

Works on a Chromebook, a computer or your phone's browser. Nothing to install yet.

2

Enter your school code, on the same website

  1. Tap You at the bottom and find Membership.
  2. Type your school code (it looks like FNDRY-7K2Q-9MXC) and tap Redeem.
  3. You'll see Code redeemed. Foundry+ access is now attached to your account.

Got your code by email? The use this link button in that email fills in the code for you. If you're signed out, tap I already have an account and sign in with the same email.

3

Get the app

  1. On the Code redeemed. screen, tap the button for your phone's app store. On a computer, scan the QR code with your phone's camera.
  2. Open the app, tap Continue with email, and sign in with the same email from Step 1.
  3. Check the Me tab. It says Unlocked with your school code. and the date your access runs through.

Use Continue with email, not Apple or Google. A different sign-in makes a new, empty account without your Portrait or your code.

Now open for you: the school rankings in Schools, Talk to me with your mentor any time, and more than one path to explore.

Prefer to stay on the website? You're already done after Step 2.

If something goes wrong

  • "That code isn't recognized." Check each letter and number, then try again.
  • "That code has already been used." Each code works once, for one student. If you didn't use it, tell your counselor.
  • "That code has expired." Ask your counselor for a new one.
  • Still locked in the app? You probably signed in a different way. Sign out in the app, then tap Continue with email and use the email from Step 1.

A school code doesn't share anything you tell Foundry with your school. Your interview and Portrait stay yours unless you add someone under Who can see me. Questions: support@foundryconnect.org · Privacy policy
