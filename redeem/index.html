Got a Foundry code from your school?

Your school is covering Foundry+ for you. Four steps and you're in. Most of the time goes to the interview, about 10 minutes.

1

Get Foundry

Android phone Get it on Google Play
iPhone App Store: coming soon

Until then, use Foundry on the web at app.foundryconnect.me. It's the whole app, and your account carries over when the iPhone app arrives.

Chromebook or computer

Use app.foundryconnect.me. Nothing to install.

2

Do your interview, then save it with your email

  1. Open Foundry and talk with your mentor. It asks about what you like and how you work.
  2. When your Portrait appears, save it: tap Continue with email. On the website, tap Sign in to save your Portrait.
  3. Type your email, then the 6-digit code we email you. We strongly suggest using a personal email address so you can continue to use Foundry after you graduate.

Use Continue with email, not Apple or Google. In Step 3 you sign in with that same email, so it has to match.

3

Enter your school code on the website

Open app.foundryconnect.me
  1. Tap I already have an account and sign in with the same email from Step 2.
  2. Tap You at the bottom and find Membership.
  3. Type your school code (it looks like FNDRY-7K2Q-9MXC) and tap Redeem.

Got your code by email? The use this link button in that email fills in the code for you. You still sign in first.

Why the website? The App Store doesn't allow school codes inside apps, so everyone enters them here. It still unlocks the app.

4

Go back to the app

  1. Close Foundry all the way, then open it again.
  2. Check the Me tab. It says Unlocked with your school code. and the date your access runs through.

Now open for you: the school rankings in Schools, Talk to me with your mentor any time, and more than one path to explore.

Using the website instead of the app? You're already done after Step 3.

If something goes wrong

  • "That code isn't recognized." Check each letter and number, then try again.
  • "That code has already been used." Each code works once, for one student. If you didn't use it, tell your counselor.
  • "That code has expired." Ask your counselor for a new one.
  • Still locked in the app? Make sure the app and the website are signed in with the same email, then close and reopen the app.

A school code doesn't share anything you tell Foundry with your school. Your interview and Portrait stay yours unless you add someone under Who can see me. Questions: hello@postmarkdigital.com · Privacy policy
