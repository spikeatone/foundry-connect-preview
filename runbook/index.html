Foundry · Demo Runbook

How to show the whole thing, end to end

The student app, the app ↔ Connect handoff, and the Foundry Index — one live walkthrough for a prospect or partner. Keep this open on a second screen and follow the phases in order.

App side · TestFlight Build 8 Connect · live at foundryconnect.me Bring · 1 iPhone + 1 laptop

The pass — who does what

📱 On the phone (the student)

  1. Enable demo mode (Me → tap “Signed in as” ×7)
  2. Interview → Portrait → sign in
  3. Pick a Path → “I did this” → reflect
  4. Who can see me → set grants → copy the link
  5. Flip a grant live · read the advisor’s note
⇄

💻 On Connect (the adult)

  1. Paste the link → the granted view of the student
  2. Watch it update ~4s after a grant flips
  3. Send a note back → lands in the app inbox
  4. The Foundry Index → a list → search / ZIP sort
  5. “How we rank” → methodology → breadcrumb back
0

Before the demo — turn on demo mode

~5 min · once

Two things in the room: a real iPhone with Build 8 (the simulator can’t do this — no real Sign in with Apple, no on-device voice) and a laptop for Connect.

On the phone, get to a signed-in account, then:

  • Go to the Me tab.
  • 📱 Phone Tap the “Signed in as…” row 7 times. A hidden “Enable demo mode” button appears. The whole row is the target — just keep tapping.
  • Tap Enable demo mode. It seeds three Connect advisors (counselor / parent / coach) and mints the demo token Connect reads. The button then reads “Demo mode on, re-sync advisors” — tap it anytime to reset advisors + grants between demos.
  • “Start fresh (demo)” also appears — it wipes the profile to blank so you can run the Interview from zero in front of the prospect. Use it right before showing the magic.
If you don’t see these: demo mode + “Start fresh” only show on a demo-enabled account and never ship to normal users. No buttons = you haven’t done the 7-tap yet.
1

The app — the magic

5–8 min

Foundry is a decade-long mentor for the teenager skipping college for a trade. It gets to know them, shows them real paths, and stays honest the whole way.

The Interview → Portrait

  • After Start fresh, the app opens on Welcome → the Interview: a warm, one-question-at-a-time conversation.
  • 📱 Phone Type a few answers — or tap the mic and talk them (on-device; nothing leaves the phone). Show both; the mic is the “kids won’t type a paragraph” story.
  • Tap “See what I’m seeing.” The constellation forms, then the Portrait: a reflection + 3–5 gentle possibilities (one “new to you”) + an honest note.
  • The Portrait offers Sign in with Apple — magic-first: the whole Interview is account-free; accounts come after the payoff.
Voice pauses are fixed in Build 7. Pause mid-sentence and earlier words are not erased — worth a beat if the prospect cares about the detail.

Real ranges, no fantasies — it never tells a kid what they want to hear.

Home — the returning student

Signed in, the app is Home with five tabs: Home · Paths · Schools · Mentor · Me.

  • Pick up a Path from a possibility → it seeds a first Step (a real next action).
  • 📱 Phone Mark a Step “I did this” → a short reflection → the mentor reacts and logs an Experience. Show the trail filling in — the read grows.
  • Schools = the Foundry Index: honest school rankings (earnings, completion, licensure pass rates — e.g. “92% FAA exam pass”). Not a career quiz — real schools on public outcome data.
2

Privacy — “Who can see me”

~2 min

The spine of the product: the student controls everything, private by default.

  • 📱 Phone Me → Who can see me. Three seeded advisors, each with per-aspect grants (Portrait, Paths, Experiences, Steps, the road ahead, and messages as its own channel).
  • Toggle one on/off — the student decides, per person, per aspect. A parent and a counselor can see completely different things.
  • Leave one advisor (say the counselor) set with Portrait + Paths + messages on for the handoff next.
3

The app ↔ Connect handoff — the “aha”

3–4 min

The money shot: the student’s phone controls what a trusted adult sees on the web, live.

  • 📱 Phone Who can see me → pick the counselor → “Copy Connect preview link.” A real URL: foundryconnect.me/?token=…&as=counselor.
  • 💻 Connect Paste it into the laptop browser → Foundry Connect opens showing that student’s real, grant-filtered data — only what the counselor may see.
  • 📱 Phone Flip a grant (turn Paths off). Within a few seconds the Connect page updates on its own (it polls every 4s). The student just changed what their counselor sees, from their pocket.
  • 💻 Connect With messages granted, use the “Reach out” box → type a note → Send. It lands in the student’s Mentor tab → “Notes from your people.” Full loop, both directions.
4

The Foundry Index in Connect — discovery

2–3 min

Connect isn’t only a mirror of the student — it’s a discovery surface for the advisor.

  • 💻 Connect Top bar → “The Foundry Index” → the catalog (21 lists: aviation, welding, electrical, cosmetology, HVAC, …).
  • Tap a list → the ranked schools with honest stat chips. An “Emerging” tier sits separately for schools without enough outcome data — never a fake rank.
  • Search — type a school / city / state to filter live.
  • Proximity — enter a ZIP → schools re-sort nearest-first with “~N mi” chips. An advisor in Dallas types their ZIP and sees the closest programs first.
  • “How we rank →” → the Methodology page: every pillar, every source with its vintage, the honesty rules. The trust play — we show our work. The breadcrumb brings you right back.
  • Light / dark — the toggle persists; set whichever reads best in the room.
5

The honesty spine — weave it throughout

the close

  • Honest by default — real ranges, real trade-offs, never a fantasy. “Emerging” instead of a faked rank; earnings as ranges; every licensure rate traces to an official government source.
  • Privacy is the foundation — student-controlled, per-aspect, private by default. Nothing shared unless the student turns it on — and off any second (show the live grant flip).
  • On-device where it counts — the voice / Interview audio never leaves the phone.
  • A decade, not a quiz — it remembers what they’ve tried, the read grows, it stays with them for years.

Reset between demos

  • 📱 Phone Me → Start fresh (demo) to run the Interview from zero again.
  • 📱 Phone Me → “Demo mode on, re-sync advisors” to reset the three advisors + grants.
  • 💻 Connect Reload the preview link — it re-reads the student’s current grants.
